Eight additions are straightforward eligible applicants in two short runs. There are 83 call entries.

Three existing eligible couples have exactly the requested restricted date options. Their members and calls are dispersed. Their six raw tentative-date pairs remain blank (`n/a`) for the model to solve. Capacity for the unique solution is reserved in the rest of the provisional calendar, and the full package contains its proof. The earlier seven spouse requests and other constructed cases remain.

Only eligibility has tentative-date columns. Every study-list applicant is represented in the diagnoses, medications, and surgical-history tabs. All 381 original MC medication formulas and cached values are preserved. Genuine source missing data remain missing. All exercise text and IDs are lowercase.

The corrected manual distinguishes enrollment ending October 30, 2027 from non-MRI data collection ending August 1, 2028. The visit interval is strictly less than three calendar months. Weekday visit 1 capacity is two people; MRI dates must satisfy both manuals. The two deliberately bad tentative bookings remain exercise inputs. Source list placement is an initial state, not a final screening decision.

939 consistency checks passed, plus independent case and calendar audits. The full package includes `reviewer/challenge_review.xlsx`, `new_applicants.csv`, source-change and identity audits, capacity proof, and an archive of the unedited original uploads. No model performance score has been measured.
